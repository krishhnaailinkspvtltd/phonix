# PHONIX GLOBAL TRADE — Import/Export Content Update (V3)

## Business direction provided by client
- Exports India-origin items internationally.
- Emphasis on dehydrated vegetables, fruits and local Indian produce.
- Includes other India-manufactured goods and product categories.
- Also discusses sourcing products from other countries for import into India.

## Updated routes
- `/` — headline, motion panels, export/import storytelling and category carousel.
- `/about` — Indian-origin trade focus and non-fabricated business overview.
- `/products` — six category filters and twelve illustrative product enquiry entries.
- `/products/:id` — product-oriented detail and quote/enquiry CTA; no false guarantees.
- `/global-trade` — export and import directions with a four-stage inquiry process.
- `/gallery` — descriptive, labelled stock image gallery.
- `/contact` — inquiry form with export/import direction and product categories.

## What is still required from the client before publication
- Confirm correct legal brand spelling (currently **PHONIX GLOBAL TRADE**).
- Actual item list (e.g. exact dehydrated vegetables/fruits), actual product images and descriptions.
- Product origin, specifications, MOQ, packing, certifications and shelf-life values.
- Countries where the business actually exports/imports; no countries are claimed in demo.
- Approved company registration and contact information.
- Form submission integration (frontend form does not send/save enquiries).
- Review photo licensing and self-host final assets.

This revision retains the prior blue/white design, loading screen and GSAP homepage scroll logic. It changes content and some image references, not the underlying brand design.
